## Scope and evidence meanings

The creator's retained Presentation report gains **Session insights**, **Export coverage CSV**
and **Download readable summary**. One strict aggregate projection supplies the panel and both
downloads. The HTML file is a self-contained reading document with semantic tables, no scripts
or remote resources. Formula-safe CSV preserves quoted authored text. Both are private copies;
they do not create a revocable share link or replace the detailed report/public-summary exports.

- **Frozen source:** session content and its original immutable publication identify the source.
  A later draft/publication cannot replace it. Session-only additions are marked when the
  original version is available; otherwise their origin stays unknown. Report V1–V7 and public
  AggregateSummary V1 are unchanged and are never regenerated by an insights read.
- **Delivery:** V6/V7 navigation distinguishes presented and skipped blocks, counting each once
  across revisits/detours. Frozen sections use those block states. Older reports provide only
  positive recorded delivery; absent history is unknown, with no invented completion percentage.
  Start time comes from actual delivery events, not lobby creation. Elapsed time is wall-clock
  time, not dwell, attendance, understanding or causation.
- **Participation:** joined identities are not verified people/devices. Accepted response records
  across activities are not unique respondents. Each activity's response rate uses its original
  reveal roster, including participants joined before that reveal and excluding later joins.
  Migration077's recorded roster is counted directly; missing legacy history is unavailable,
  while an explicit empty roster is a known zero. Final session size is never substituted.
- **Opinions:** cached multi-select/ranking results retain their respondent/weighted-point basis.
  Ordinary single-choice polls and ratings use a bounded counts-only fallback where the historical
  report did not store a distribution. Frozen choice/rating labels supply display text. Counts
  must match the report's accepted respondents and known buckets; inconsistent or missing evidence
  is explicitly unavailable. Opinions and session-only unscored Quick Checks gain no accuracy or
  participant score. Assessment accuracy stays in the original detailed report, where its original
  denominator is preserved.
- **Recovery:** the denominator is initially incorrect participants with an accepted linked recheck;
  the numerator is those who recovered. It does not include initially incorrect participants who
  never answered the recheck, and it does not establish long-term learning or causal impact.
- **Q&A:** a separate current moderation snapshot counts only published/answered questions and
  their votes. Pending/dismissed/removed questions and all question bodies stay excluded. Counts
  carry their own timestamp/sequence and can change after finish. A final sequence fence retries
  once, then marks Q&A unavailable without inventing zero or hiding other valid insights.

Positive activity and Q&A samples below five are suppressed. Opinion distributions and recovery
rates require at least five accepted or paired responses. Known zero, suppressed, unavailable and
skipped states remain distinct. No participant IDs, aliases, response bodies,
moderated text, word-cloud terms, presenter notes, answer keys, private branch details or tokens
enter the new DTO or downloads. Authored public labels/titles remain visible, escaped as data.

## Access, bounds and cost

`GET /v1/presentation-sessions/:id/insights` requires current creator authentication and current
workspace membership. It accepts compatible historical reports even if creation is paused.
Participant/stage/Companion bearers supply no creator authority. Session/report identity,
workspace deletion, actor/session validity, finished state and effective retention are rechecked
before delivery. Effective retention is the earlier session/report expiry. Responses use no-store,
nosniff and no-referrer headers. Pending/failed reports remain pending/failed without a new job.

There are at most 200 blocks/selectors, 1 MiB of serialized source before parsing/aggregate reads,
1 MiB of output, 60 reads per actor per minute and bounded distribution buckets. Reveals are
selected in one pass without copying/sorting the timeline. Workspace-scoped database queries
return only roster counts, opinion bucket counts or public Q&A counts/sequence, never raw rows
or identities to the application. The opinion query uses a 1.5-second statement timeout and no
retry queue; unavailable counts stay explicit. Forced RLS and retention remain enforced.

No migration, persisted insights document, new service/dependency, provider call, AI narrative,
per-view rendering job or entitlement is added. Operational metrics record bounded read outcomes,
duration and response bytes without tenant/session/participant labels. These measure resource use;
they do not establish a subscription price or production operating-cost claim.

## Review follow-up: legacy snapshots and large sequences

The opinion query now defaults an absent embedded `schemaVersion` to 1 when choosing legacy
identity semantics, matching the read upcaster without rewriting stored snapshots. A restricted
PostgreSQL regression verifies ordinary poll/rating buckets, literal legacy choice matching,
rejection of a modern semantic-identity selector and unchanged snapshot bytes/publication hash/report.

Reveal selectors now use PostgreSQL `bigint[]`, matching the timeline sequence column. The shared
Memory/PostgreSQL roster case includes sequence 2,147,483,648 and preserves the exact recorded roster.

an empty result and the reveal lookup overflowed `integer`. After the fixes, run `e3b26de501` passed
all five selected PostgreSQL cases; all owned resources were cleaned up and the 14 original
containers remained unchanged. No migration, stored-content/report mutation or public contract
change is required.

`VITEST_MAX_WORKERS=1 pnpm check` passed again after both fixes, including the complete package
tests and production builds. Private log: `/tmp/pollingpops-e09-review-fix-check.log`.
