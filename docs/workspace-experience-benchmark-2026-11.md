# Polling Pops workspace experience benchmark

Access date: 2026-11-17 · Repository baseline: `origin/main` at `6fd5441`.

The requested direction is a simpler, polished commercial workspace with compatible feature
additions delivered in small increments. Mentimeter offers a useful creation-to-results lifecycle;
Wayground for Business illustrates task hierarchy, library organization and preview before reuse.
Polling Pops should improve discovery of its substantial existing capability before treating this
research as a feature-parity program. The companion
[workspace experience backlog](workspace-experience-backlog-2026-11.md) defines the selected slices.

## Evidence and limits

- **Marketing:** public product pages describe positioning and advertised capabilities.
- **Help-documented:** first-party instructions describe workflows and restrictions. They are
  vendor documentation, not independently tested behavior.
- **Screenshot observation:** the supplied Wayground Business images show a particular interface
  and offer. They do not establish current availability, entitlements or complete behavior.
- **Repository baseline:** the supplied source inventory and linked engineering records describe
  implemented foundations, including gated paths. They do not establish hosted release readiness.

Public pages were read without account creation, purchases or authenticated product trials. No
competitor performance, accessibility conformance or customer outcomes were independently measured.
No customer interviews were collected. Recommendations are original product hypotheses. Competitor
screenshots, identities, templates, copy and expressive assets are not stored or reproduced here.
The current [Wayground Canada homepage](https://wayground.com/ca/home) markets education; Business
screenshots and Business help are a distinct comparison context.

### Supplied screenshot observations

The Business home shows persistent navigation and global search, one dominant topic/document
creation composer, four quick actions, then templates. The Library empty state offers quiz/lesson
creation and templates alongside type/state/recency controls. A large template dialog combines
search/type/category controls, a list, detailed preview and persistent use action; cards disclose
format and question/slide counts.

These observations support task hierarchy and preview before creating a copy. They do not justify
copying promotional banners, duplicating search fields or adopting a large dialog with two
independent scrolling regions. Responsive inline discovery can serve the same job.

## Pricing and documentation uncertainty

[Mentimeter pricing](https://www.mentimeter.com/plans) advertises 50 free participants/month,
paid imports/exports, Pro design/team tools and custom Enterprise. Dollar amounts did not render
in the retrieved page. Its [free-account help](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/1258367-what-is-included-in-the-free-account)
instead distinguishes accounts created before November 4, 2026 from those created after it:
older accounts have the participant quota, newer accounts unlimited participants with restricted
post-session results/AI. The boundary date itself and account assignment remain ambiguous.
[Paid-plan help](https://help.mentimeter.com/en/articles/5938993-which-plan-is-right-for-you)
also specifies 20,000 participants, or 2,000 for quizzes, despite unlimited marketing.

Form help is internally inconsistent: its opening labels scales forthcoming while later sections
describe scales, ranking, fields and video. Public tools, Help Center instructions and authenticated
availability have not been reconciled by an account trial.

[Wayground Business plan help](https://forbusiness-help.wayground.com/support/solutions/articles/158000411055-participant-limits-in-wayground-for-business-plans)
lists USD 125/month alongside its individual Essential offering, USD 900 billed annually alongside
Essential's “up to three seats,” and USD 1,500 billed annually alongside Pro's five-seat minimum.
The table does not establish whether those annual figures are per seat or account totals, so they
must not be treated as confirmed multi-seat purchase totals. It lists 150 Essential and 1,000 Pro
participants/session. The supplied
two-seat, $25/seat promotion conflicts with that public snapshot; currency, eligibility and timing
were not established. Neither competitor's pricing is a Polling Pops pricing recommendation.

## Polling Pops baseline and useful gaps

The baseline already has Home metrics/recent work/action guidance; Library Round/Presentation/Survey
tabs, filters, favorites, folders, grid/list, archive/delete and organization/shared permissions.
See [Home behavior](home-workspace-overview.md) and
[shared-library boundaries](organization-shared-library.md). Existing templates cover 36 Round,
21 Presentation and 17 Survey scenarios across ten locales, with preview and independent copies.
Native Presentation authoring, import, stage, review, reading, practice and exports already exist;
Rounds have nine response families and live word clouds. Missing discovery is not missing capability.

| Evaluation order           | Bounded candidate                                                                                        | Existing roadmap relationship                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First slice                | Simplify Home hierarchy; make template discovery, format links and preview-to-copy transitions reliable. | Existing workspace/template workflows; companion backlog owns implementation status.                                       |
| Next workflow slice        | Mixed Library server search/sort with explicit scope, pagination and permissions.                        | Extend current Library rather than replacing typed views or organization sharing.                                          |
| Selected feature increment | Richer Survey logic; allocation, 2×2 or image-choice only when a demonstrated job needs it.              | Extend IDEA-004 / R2-03 one surface and complete journey at a time.                                                        |
| Separate discovery         | Recurring check-ins, report comparisons and a narrow moderator pass.                                     | Live Pulse is not recurrence; compare IDEA-008, IDEA-015 / R2-10 and IDEA-014 / R2-09.                                     |
| Later expansion            | Native integrations and curated organization templates.                                                  | IDEA-002/007 and R2-02; extend current organization publications. Curation is distinct from IDEA-003's public marketplace. |

Acceptance should prove keyboard/mobile operation, ten-locale copy, clear loading/empty/error states,
tenant-safe search/counts, current role checks and independent reusable copies. Publishing, sharing,
imports, comparisons and recurring rounds must retain immutable source/version binding and existing
retention rules. New contracts remain additive or receive explicit versions and upcasters.

Use existing [IDEA records](future-product-ideas.md) and
[R2 selection rules](competitor-backlog-2026-10-27.md), not duplicate feature programs. Observe
unassisted discovery and complete workflows with authorized participants before claiming customer
benefit. This benchmark neither supplies customer demand nor closes release, native-language,
physical-device or assistive-technology acceptance gates.
